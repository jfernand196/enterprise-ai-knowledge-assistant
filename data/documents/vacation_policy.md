---
title: Vacation Policy
category: hr
---
Employees receive 15 paid vacation days during their first year of employment. After two years of continuous service, the annual allowance increases to 20 paid vacation days.

Vacation requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance through the HR portal. Managers should approve or decline a request within 5 business days.

Unused vacation days do not roll over to the next calendar year except when a manager freeze prevented the employee from taking time off. In that case, up to 5 days may be carried over.

The company does not pay out unused vacation except upon termination, and only for days already accrued in the current year.
