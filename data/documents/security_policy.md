---
title: Security Policy
category: security
---
All employees must enable multi-factor authentication on company email, VPN, and Databricks workspaces. Passwords must not be shared, written on paper, or stored in unencrypted files.

Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security within 2 hours.

Production data, including customer records and internal documents used by the AI assistant, must not be copied to personal machines. Access to production systems requires an approved ticket and time-limited credentials.
